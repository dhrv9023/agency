The 4 Objections Every HVAC Owner Will Throw at You (And How to Destroy Them)
Objection 1: "We already have ServiceTitan / Jobber / Housecall Pro."
Rebuttal: "ServiceTitan is great for dispatching technicians and billing once a job is booked. Objection 2: "Our office receptionist handles all customer inquiries."
Rebuttal: "Your receptionist does a great job between 8 AM and 5 PM. But what happens to the quote requests that come through your website between 6 PM and 7 AM, or all weekend? Objection 3: "I don't trust an AI to give wrong prices to my customers."
Rebuttal: "That's exactly why our system is strictly hardcoded NEVER to quote prices or diagnose mechanical issues. Its only job is to collect the customer's name, address, urgency, and book a diagnostic slot so your licensed technician can give the quote in person. It protects your brand 100%."
Objection 4: "We get enough work from referrals and word-of-mouth."
