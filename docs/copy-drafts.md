## 2. Solution Page: Workflow Automation (`public/solutions/ai-automation.html`)

**H1**: Workflow automation for routine business operations  
**Subhead**: Connect your everyday tools, eliminate duplicate data entry, and let your staff focus on work that requires human judgment.  

### The Problem in Plain Words
Every business has invisible time sinks. Staff spend hours copying data from emails into spreadsheets, downloading PDFs to upload into accounting software, and emailing colleagues for routine updates. These tasks are slow, error-prone, and pull experienced staff away from customers.

### What We Build
We build secure, background connections between your existing business tools. When an event happens in one tool, our software validates the data, applies your business rules, and updates your other systems immediately.

### What a Normal Day Looks Like
1. An incoming order or inquiry arrives by email or web form.
2. The automation system reads the details, verifies required fields, and checks your database.
3. The system creates the customer record, notifies the right team member, and drafts the next step.
4. Your staff member reviews and approves the action with a single click.

### What We Need From You
- A 45-minute walkthrough of how your team currently completes the task.
- Read-only access to test accounts for the tools involved.
- Three to five examples of normal cases and two examples of unusual exceptions.

### What This Is Not (Honest Limits)
- This is not a generic off-the-shelf bot that promises to run your whole company.
- We do not replace core staff. We remove tedious busywork so your staff can accomplish more.
- We do not deploy autonomous actions that move money or delete records without human review.

### Typical Timeline
- **Discovery and workflow mapping**: 3 business days.
- **System build and testing**: 10 to 15 business days.
- **Staff training and production launch**: 3 business days.

### How Success Is Measured
- Total hours saved per week on manual entry.
- Response time reduction on customer requests.
- Error rate dropped to under 0.1% on repetitive data entry.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (`public/faq.html` & homepage preview)

**H1**: Frequently asked questions  
**Subhead**: Clear answers to common questions about our services, process, security, and pricing.  

1. **Do we need an internal IT team to work with you?**  
   No. We manage all technical setup, coding, and software connections. We explain everything in plain business terms and train your team directly.

2. **How long does a typical project take from start to finish?**  
   A single-workflow system typically takes two to four weeks. More complex integrations across multiple departments take six to eight weeks.

3. **What happens to our company and client data?**  
   Your data remains yours. We never use client data to train public AI models. We configure enterprise privacy agreements with model providers so your data is encrypted, processed securely, and never stored for training.

4. **Will AI software make mistakes with our customers or numbers?**  
   AI models can make errors if left unchecked. That is why we never build unconstrained systems. We build strict guardrails: if information does not meet predefined certainty rules, the system flags it for a human team member to review before any action occurs.

5. **Will this replace our staff?**  
   No. Our clients use automation to eliminate the repetitive tasks their staff dislike doing, such as copying numbers between systems or answering the same routine question dozens of times a day. Your team gains time to focus on complex client service and business growth.

6. **What tools can you connect with?**  
   We connect with modern cloud platforms including QuickBooks, Xero, HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Stripe, and custom internal SQL databases. If your tool has an API or webhook support, we can connect it.

7. **What if we want to cancel or change our system later?**  
   You own your systems and configurations. If you ever decide to stop working with us, your software connections continue running in your own accounts. We provide complete technical documentation so any qualified engineer can maintain them.

8. **How quickly do you respond when something needs attention?**  
   We monitor all active client automations continuously. For support inquiries, we reply within one business day during standard business hours.
