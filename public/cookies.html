1. Client-Side Storage Technologies

When you visit websites, modern browsers store technical data on your local device to enable navigation, preserve user preferences, and secure transactions. These technologies include:

  • Cookies: Small alphanumeric text files placed in your browser directory by a web server.
  • Local Storage (localStorage): Browser-managed key-value storage that persists across sessions until explicitly cleared.
  • Session Storage (sessionStorage): Ephemeral key-value storage that is automatically and permanently expunged as soon as you close the active browser tab.

2. Our Minimalist Approach to Storage

VARAH SYSTEMS does not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or surveillance pixels on this website. Our marketing site sets zero HTTP tracking cookies.

All storage items currently utilized on our marketing website fall under the Strictly Necessary exemption defined by Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC) as technical necessities to deliver the specific functional services requested by the visitor (e.g., maintaining UI contrast themes and securing inquiry forms from automated spam).

4. How to Manage or Update Your Choices

You can review or adjust your consent choices at any time directly on this website. Click the button below to open your preference panel:

Our consent system enforces the following rules:

  • Strictly Necessary: Always active. Disabling this would break form delivery and security verifications.
  • Performance & Analytics: Blocked by default. When active, measures aggregate technical site health.
  • Marketing & Targeting: Blocked by default. We do not currently operate third-party ad pixels.

5. Browser-Level Storage Controls

Most modern web browsers allow you to manage or delete stored data through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → Clear browsing data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Apple Safari: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → Remove All Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.

Please note that completely blocking all browser storage may degrade functional site behaviors, such as theme selection persistence.

6. Global Privacy Control (GPC)

Our consent architecture natively honors the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. If your browser transmits a valid GPC header (navigator.globalPrivacyControl === true), our system automatically defaults all non-essential analytics and marketing categories to disabled without requiring manual configuration.
