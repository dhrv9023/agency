Data Protection & Governance

Security & Data Handling Practices

We build enterprise automations using verified open-source models, encrypted data flows, and strict human safety boundaries. Here is exactly how we handle your company data.

1. Open-Source Model Isolation

Whenever possible, we deploy robust open-source models in isolated cloud environments. Your documents, customer messages, and financial data are processed directly inside your private tenant without being sent to third-party public AI aggregators.

Where proprietary commercial models are specifically requested, we configure enterprise API agreements that strictly prohibit the model provider from retaining or training on your information.

2. Zero Model Training Policy

We never use client operational data, files, customer records, or internal prompts to train public AI models. Your business processes, financial metrics, and customer communications remain strictly your proprietary intellectual property.

3. Encryption and Access Control

All data transmitted between your business tools and our automation software is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 protocols. Data stored at rest is encrypted using AES-256 standards.

We enforce strict role-based access control. Only authorized members of your team receive administrative access to system dashboards and management controls.

4. Human-in-the-Loop Guardrails

We never deploy automations with unconstrained authority to alter records or execute high-value transactions. Every workflow contains predefined boundary checks:

  • Confidence thresholds: Inquiries or invoice readings below 95% certainty automatically pause for staff review.
  • Transaction thresholds: Financial amounts exceeding your approved budget limit trigger a human sign-off request.
  • Immediate fallback: If an external software API fails, the system logs the incident safely and notifies your team without dropping data.

5. Complete Client System Control

You maintain full administrative control of your software accounts, credentials, and data flows. VARAH SYSTEMS operates as your engineering partner to maintain and optimize the system under our service agreement, but your business data remains in your control at all times.

Need a specific compliance review?

We are happy to review your data handling requirements and provide an architectural diagram before any build begins.
