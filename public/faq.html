Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, plain-language answers to common questions about our services, timelines, pricing, security, and delivery process.

Do we need technical employees to work with you?

No. We handle all technical architecture, software development, and tool connections. We connect with the software you already use and train your team directly so anyone can use the system comfortably.

How long does a project take from start to launch?

A single-workflow automation sprint takes between 2 and 4 weeks. Multi-system setups across multiple operational departments take between 6 and 8 weeks. We provide a firm delivery timeline before starting.

What happens to our company and client data?

Your data remains completely confidential. We use private and open-source models deployed securely within your designated environment. We enforce strict agreements ensuring your business information is never used to train public models.

Will AI make mistakes with our business records?

AI models can make errors if unconstrained. That is why we engineer strict rule-based guardrails. If data confidence is low or values fall outside normal parameters, the system flags the item for human staff review before taking action.

Will automation replace our team?

No. Our systems eliminate repetitive clerical tasks like manual invoice entry or routing routine queries. Your team gains time back to focus on high-value client relationships and core operations.

How does pricing work for Indian and global clients?

We provide fixed build fees and monthly maintenance retainers. For Indian businesses, we quote transparently in Indian Rupees (INR). For international businesses, we quote in US Dollars (USD). You receive a complete scope document before any billing occurs.

What if we decide to stop working together later?

You own complete control of your operational systems, credentials, and data flows. We maintain the underlying engine under our monthly retainer, but there is zero lock-in. If you cancel, your data and software connections remain fully operational in your accounts.

How quickly do you reply when support is needed?

We reply within one business day for all client requests. Active client systems include automated monitoring that alerts our engineers if an external tool API changes.

Have a question about your specific workflow?

Tell us about your current manual bottlenecks. We will review your process and explain if automation makes sense for your business.
