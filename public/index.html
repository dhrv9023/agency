05 // Clear Answers

Questions business owners ask before starting.

Clear, honest answers on delivery timelines, pricing models, and ongoing maintenance.

How long does a typical build take?
A single-workflow system (such as WhatsApp support, voice call answering, or lead routing) takes 2 to 4 weeks from audit to launch. Multi-system enterprise deployments take 4 to 8 weeks.
How does pricing work for Indian and international clients?
Every engagement is scoped with a transparent, fixed build fee plus an optional monthly maintenance retainer. We scope in Rupees (₹ INR) for Indian businesses and US Dollars ($ USD) for global businesses.
Do we need to replace any of our existing software?
No. We build directly around your existing tools, including WhatsApp Business, Twilio, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Google Workspace, QuickBooks, or custom internal databases.
