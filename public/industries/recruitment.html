Industry Focus // Staffing & Talent

Recruitment & Talent AI Systems

Transform recruitment pipelines with intelligent resume parsing, deterministic skill matching, multi-channel candidate re-engagement, and automated interview coordination.

01 // THE FRICTION

High Volume, Inconsistent Triage

Recruiters spend hundreds of hours manually skimming mismatched resumes, cross-referencing profiles, and chasing candidates via endless email threads to schedule initial screening calls.
02 // ARCHITECTURE

Talent Pipeline Orchestrator

Ingests applications from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, or email. Parses technical skills, past tenure, and certifications against a defined role scorecard. Automatically initiates async screening questions and writes back evaluations directly to the ATS.
RECRUITMENT STACK: Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby API • Contextual Resume Parser • Objective Scorecard Engine • Automated Calendar Dispatch
03 // OUTCOMES

Talent Acquisition Impact

  • • Accelerated Time-to-Screen: Top applicants receive engagement within minutes of submitting.
  • • Re-activated Candidate Database: Re-engages qualified past applicants by matching against new open requisitions.
  • • Standardized Evaluation: Eliminates human bias and fatigue in early triage stages.
