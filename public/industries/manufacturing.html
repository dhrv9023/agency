Manufacturing AI Systems

Connect plant floor operations, supplier RFQ parsing, bill-of-lading extraction, and enterprise ERP synchronization into a unified, high-reliability automation pipeline.

01 // THE BOTTLENECK

Supplier Data Inconsistency & ERP Lag

Procurement and plant managers struggle with hundreds of unstructured PDF quotes, packing slips, customs declarations, and material safety data sheets (MSDS) arriving from dozens of overseas suppliers.
02 // ARCHITECTURE

Industrial Ingestion Engine

Automatically captures supplier emails, extracts part numbers, quantities, lead times, and unit pricing into validated JSON schemas, and reconciles line-items against enterprise ERPs (SAP, NetSuite, Epicor).
SUPPLY CHAIN STACK: SAP / NetSuite API • Multi-Modal OCR • Part Number Matcher • EDI / Kafka Streams
03 // OUTCOMES

Supply Chain Operational Impact

  • • 95% Faster RFQ Processing: Vendor bids parsed and normalized within seconds for rapid supplier comparison.
  • • Real-time Inventory Visibility: Automatically updates warehouse stock on receipt of bill of lading.
  • • Reduced Stockouts: Proactive alerts when lead times or supplier delivery commitments deviate.
