Real Estate AI Systems

Automate tenant acquisition, 24/7 inquiry qualification, self-guided showing coordination, and lease document extraction across residential and commercial portfolios.

01 // THE BOTTLENECK

Inquiry Overload & Tour Scheduling

Property managers and leasing agents spend 50%+ of their day answering Zillow/MLS inquiries, re-checking tenant criteria (income, credit score, move-in dates), and coordinating tour walkthroughs across disparate calendars.
02 // ARCHITECTURE

Intelligent Leasing Concierge

An omnichannel AI agent connected to your MLS/AppFolio/Yardi database. It validates prospective tenant qualifications via SMS and webchat, confirms lockbox or agent-guided showing appointments, and syncs status instantly.
INTEGRATION STACK: AppFolio / Yardi / Buildium • Twilio SMS / Voice • Showing Time Sync • Multi-Modal Lease Parser
