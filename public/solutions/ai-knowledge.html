Solution 06 // Neural Information Retrieval

AI Knowledge Systems

Transform company documentation, Google Drive archives, Notion workspaces, and SQL databases into an accurate, grounded retrieval engine with source citations.

01 // INFORMATION SILOS

The Hidden Cost of Search

Knowledge workers spend up to 20% of their workweek hunting for internal SOPs, pricing sheets, compliance rules, and past project data. Keyword searches fail on synonyms and lack contextual synthesis.
02 // ARCHITECTURE

Hybrid Semantic RAG Pipeline

We engineer multi-stage retrieval pipelines: combining lexical BM25 matching with dense vector embeddings and cross-encoder re-ranking. Models are strictly constrained to cite verbatim sources and explicitly declare when information is missing.
RAG ARCHITECTURE: Vector DBs (Qdrant/Pinecone) • Contextual Chunking • Cross-Encoder Re-Ranking • Strict Citation Guardrails
03 // ENTERPRISE SECURITY

Permissions & Privacy

  • • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Queries strictly filter results based on the individual user's verified permission level.
  • • Private Cloud & On-Premises: Can be hosted entirely within your VPC with zero public model training on your proprietary data.
  • • Live Sync Connectors: Automatically updates vector indexes whenever Notion pages, Google Docs, or PDF files are modified.
