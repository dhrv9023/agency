3. Personal Data We Collect & Why

We collect personal data strictly when you provide it to us or as necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the site. We do not engage in behavioral tracking or cross-contextual behavioral advertising.

A. Inbound Project Inquiries (Contact Form)

When you complete our technical scoping intake form (contact.html), we collect:

  • Identity & Contact: Full name, professional work email address, company name, and company website URL.
  • Project Requirements: Operational bottleneck description, software stack and databases, primary service of interest, budget range, target implementation timeline, and preferred consultation meeting times.
  • Attribution Context (Consent Gated): If Performance & Analytics consent is explicitly granted, we capture campaign parameters (UTM source, medium, campaign, term, content), referral origin (with query parameters and fragments stripped), and landing page. If consent has not been granted, attribution fields remain strictly null.

Purpose: To evaluate your automation requirements, determine architectural feasibility, respond to your inquiry, and deliver technical consulting proposals within our stated response timeframe (one business day).

B. Security, Rate Limiting & Anti-Abuse Telemetry

To protect our server infrastructure from automated spam, brute-force form submission, and denial-of-service attempts, our backend API processes:

  • Cryptographic IP Hash: Your IP address is processed using a one-way HMAC-SHA256 hashing algorithm salted with a secret server-side key. Your raw IP address is never stored in our application database tables.
  • Submission Timestamps: Stored to enforce a rate limit sliding window of no more than 5 requests per 15-minute interval.
  • Anti-Abuse Log: If a submission triggers our honeypot trap, violates rate limits, or fails validation, we log the IP hash, submission reason, email domain (e.g. @company.com), and message snippet truncated to 200 characters. Zero names, phone numbers, or full email addresses are recorded in security logs.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile Verification: Ephemeral challenge response tokens verified with Cloudflare, Inc. to detect automated bot activity without intrusive image puzzles.

C. Client-Side Browser Storage

Our website uses browser local storage and session storage strictly for functional, security, and consent-gated purposes:

  • agency_theme & varah_admin_theme (LocalStorage): Stores your selected UI theme mode (light or dark) across page navigations.
  • varah_consent_state (LocalStorage): Records your consent preferences (Necessary, Analytics, Marketing) and timestamp for 180 days.
  • varah_lead_submitted (SessionStorage): Ephemeral flag to prevent direct unauthorized access to our confirmation page (thank-you.html). Automatically destroyed when you close the browser tab.
  • varah_first_touch_attribution (SessionStorage): Ephemeral campaign attribution parameters captured strictly upon consent. Destroyed on tab close.
  • varah_lead_tracked (SessionStorage): Prevents duplicate conversion event dispatches on page reloads. Destroyed on tab close.
  • varah_cta_dismissed (SessionStorage): Remembers visitor dismissal of the mobile action bar during the active session. Destroyed on tab close.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases recognized by the GDPR:

  • Pre-Contractual Steps (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing your project scoping inquiry, estimating sprint scopes, and scheduling discovery consultations at your request.
  • Legitimate Interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): Protecting our digital infrastructure against denial-of-service attacks, brute-force spam, and fraudulent bot submissions through salted IP rate limiting and CAPTCHA challenge verification.
  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): For optional performance analytics or measurement scripts (which remain disabled by default and only execute if explicitly granted).
  • Legal Obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)): Retaining necessary records where mandated by applicable commercial or tax legislation.

8. Your Legal Rights & How to Exercise Them

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have specific statutory rights concerning your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request that we delete your personal data where no overriding legal basis for retention exists.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time using our on-page control.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to file a complaint with your competent supervisory authority (for example, the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK, your respective EU Data Protection Authority, or regional privacy commissioner).

To exercise any of these rights, contact our privacy team at privacy@domain-pending.invalid. We verify requester identity before disclosing or deleting data and respond within 30 days.

10. Children's Data

Our services and website are directed strictly at enterprise businesses, commercial operators, and adult working professionals. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or retain personal data from children under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information through our contact form, please notify privacy@domain-pending.invalid and we will immediately expunge the record.

11. Technical & Operational Security Measures

We implement technical and organizational security measures appropriate to the nature of our data processing:

  • Transport Encryption: All public marketing traffic and API transmissions are strictly forced over HTTPS utilizing TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • IP Hashing Salt: IP addresses processed for rate limiting are transformed via keyed HMAC-SHA256 hashes, preventing reverse lookup.
  • Zero-PII Abuse Tracking: Security tables record only email domains (e.g. @example.com) and truncated text snippets (maximum 200 characters), excluding submitter identities.
  • Prepared Database Queries: All database queries are executed via parameterized Prisma ORM operations to eliminate SQL injection vulnerabilities.
