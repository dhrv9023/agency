Solution 03 // Real-Time Audio

AI Voice Systems

Ultra-low latency conversational telephony designed for inbound service triage, emergency dispatching, appointment booking, and automated phone outreach.

01 // TELEPHONY LATENCY

Sub-500ms Turn-Taking

Standard IVRs and early voice bots suffered from awkward pauses and robotic cadence. We engineer full-duplex WebRTC and SIP pipelines with real-time speech-to-speech models, natural interruption handling, and acoustic noise suppression.
02 // ARCHITECTURE

Integrated Telephony Pipeline

Calls are streamed directly from PSTN/SIP trunks to neural speech processing units. Intent is recognized in real-time, querying database availability via function calls during the conversation and writing back transcripts directly to your CRM.
VOICE STACK: Twilio / SIP Trunking • WebRTC Audio Streaming • Neural Turn Detection • Real-time Tool Dispatch
03 // APPLICATIONS

Voice Automation In Action

  • • 24/7 Inbound Intake: Answers every call on ring one, collects key parameters, checks calendar availability, and confirms bookings.
  • • Service Dispatching: Evaluates emergency urgency for field services (HVAC, plumbing, logistics) and dispatches technicians autonomously.
  • • Warm Transfers: Seamlessly bridges callers to human specialists with a complete live summary displayed on the agent's screen.
