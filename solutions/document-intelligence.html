01 // THE EXTRACTION GAP

Beyond Template OCR

Traditional OCR relies on rigid coordinates that break the moment a vendor changes layout or formatting. Modern document intelligence uses multi-modal visual LLMs that comprehend semantic layout, tables, nested line-items, and handwriting effortlessly.
02 // ARCHITECTURE

Validation & Reconciliation Pipeline

Documents arriving via email, S3 uploads, or mobile scans are rendered at high DPI, processed through multi-modal parsing models, and verified with strict schema rules (e.g. verifying tax sum equals subtotal plus sales tax before writing to ERP).
