AI Customer Support Systems

Resolve repetitive tier-1 support tickets automatically, execute order modifications via API, and intelligently route high-stakes inquiries to human specialists.

01 // THE DEFECT WITH CHATBOTS

Moving Beyond Canned Answers

Standard chatbots frustrate customers with generic FAQ links. Our support systems act as authenticated operational agents: verifying user identity, checking order databases, processing refunds within policy rules, and updating tickets in Zendesk, Intercom, or Gorgias.
02 // ARCHITECTURE

Grounded Triage & Action Engine

Incoming tickets are classified by sentiment, urgency, and category. Routine requests trigger verified transactional API mutations. Ambiguous or emotionally charged tickets are packaged with suggested responses and routed to your senior team.
